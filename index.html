We build the intelligence layer for the physical world.

Cognion Global was founded on a simple premise: infrastructure, cities and large-scale operations generate enormous amounts of data, but very little of it becomes a decision in time to matter. We close that gap.

Who we are

Cognion Global is a technology integration company. We bring together digital twins, AI, geospatial intelligence, computer vision, cloud and cybersecurity into a single operating capability for governments, infrastructure operators and enterprises.

We are not a staffing company, and we don't ask a client to become an expert in five technology categories to get value from us. We architect the solution, select and manage the right specialist partners for each component, and take responsibility for the outcome.

What we believe

One operating picture, not ten dashboards

Most organizations already have GIS, CCTV, IoT sensors and enterprise systems. The problem is that none of it talks to each other in time to act on.

A digital twin should predict something

A 3D model of a city is a visualization. A digital twin that anticipates congestion, risk or failure is a decision-support system.

Technology should be assembled, not reinvented

We work with best-in-class partners across geospatial platforms, computer vision, cloud and cybersecurity.

Where we work

India, the GCC, and the corridor between them — with governments and enterprises operating mission-critical physical environments.
